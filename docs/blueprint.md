# **App Name**: R2D2 Central

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Content Hub: A centralized system for managing and displaying news articles, videos, team history, and partner/sponsor information. Supports various content types and media.
- AI News Summarizer: Utilize an AI tool to automatically generate concise and engaging summaries for news articles, enhancing content discoverability on the homepage and news listing pages.
- Educational Course Tracker: Allows users to enroll in robotics courses and track their completion progress with a percentage indicator within the Educational Material section.
- Interactive Team R2D2 Showcase: Dedicated navigation and sections to present 'What is R2D2', competitor lists, official guides for future competitions, and an historical archive.
- Homepage Section Previews: A dynamic scrolling experience on the homepage that provides introductory summaries and direct access to News, Videos, Team R2D2, and Educational sections.
- Partners & Sponsors Directory: A visual section or dedicated page to highlight team partners and sponsors, featuring logos and external link integrations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep corporate/sports blue (#003399) used for major headers, navigation bars, and section backgrounds.
- Accent color: Vibrant yellow (#FFCC00) used for call-to-action buttons, high-priority interactive elements, and highlights, replacing the previous red.
- Neutral colors: Clean white (#FFFFFF) for backgrounds, light grey (#F5F7FA) for surfaces, and dark navy (#0E1A2F) for main text.
- Main font family: 'Poppins' (geometric sans-serif). Hierarchy includes H1 at 32px Bold, H2 at 24px SemiBold, and Body at 14px with 1.6 line height.
- Clean, geometric icons featuring circular micro-buttons for carousel controls and chevron arrows for navigation.
- A card-based interface with a 1200px max width and 24px grid gaps. Sections alternate between clean white and deep blue blocks to define distinct modules.
- Smooth scrolling section reveals and a micro-bounce effect on the yellow fixed sticky widget located in the bottom-right corner.
